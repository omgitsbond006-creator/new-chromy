Privacy Policy

App: Orbiquo · Effective date: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Orbiquo ("the app", "the game") handles information. By installing or using the app, you agree to the practices described below.

In short: Orbiquo does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. The game works fully offline and all game progress stays on your device.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data, and it does not require an account or sign-in.

2. Information stored on your device

To make the game work, the following data is saved locally on your device only:

  • Your best score and number of games played
  • The number of gems you have collected and the orb skins you have unlocked
  • Your settings (music, sound effects and vibration on/off)

This data is never transmitted off your device. You can delete it at any time from Settings > Reset Progress inside the app, or by uninstalling the app.

3. Internet access, analytics and advertising

Orbiquo does not require an internet connection. The app does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking, crash-reporting or social media SDKs, and it does not use cookies or similar technologies.

4. Permissions

The app does not request any sensitive device permissions. Haptic feedback (vibration) uses standard system feedback and can be turned off in the in-app settings.

5. Sharing of information

Because we do not collect any personal information, we do not share, rent or sell personal information to anyone.

6. Third-party services

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information about app downloads and device performance under its own privacy policy, which you can read at https://policies.google.com/privacy. We do not receive any personal information from Google about you.

7. Children's privacy

Orbiquo is suitable for players of all ages. Since the app does not collect any personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

8. Data security

All game data remains in the app's private storage on your device, protected by your device's built-in security. No data is transmitted over the network.

9. Your rights

Since no personal data is collected or processed by us, there is no personal data for us to access, correct or delete. You remain in full control of the game data on your device and can erase it at any time as described above.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact & support

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or need support with the game, please contact us through the Orbiquo listing page on the Google Play Store. Open the app's page on Google Play and use the contact details shown in the "App support" section.
